IKL CRM - acceptance test and training plan

For the person IKL nominates to accept the system, and for the staff who will use it. Final delivery: Monday 30 November 2026. Print this page and tick as you go.

How the acceptance test works

  • One tester per role (Head of Department, Sales, Marketing) works through that role's scenarios on the system with a real login. Scenarios marked "all roles" are done once.
  • For each scenario: follow the steps, compare with the expected result, write Pass or Fail and a short comment.
  • Email and WhatsApp run in demonstration mode until the IT department connects the real services; those scenarios are accepted on what is recorded, and repeated once the services are live.
  • Accepted when every scenario is Pass, or each Fail has a written decision (fix before go-live, or accept and fix later).
