IKL CRM - one-page overview

One CRM for IKL's corporate training and open-enrollment programs. Status after Sprint 3 (17 Nov 2026). Final delivery: 30 Nov 2026.

How the system works

1. Leads come inWebsite forms, Excel import, by hand
2. Sales works themInquiries, proposals, invoice references
3. Programs runCatalogue, batches, registrations, attendance
4. People completeAlumni, certificate record, feedback
5. Marketing follows upEmail, WhatsApp, automations

Every person has one record (lead, participant, alumnus). Each role sees only what it is allowed to; every action is checked on the server.

Who uses what

RoleMain work
Head of DepartmentDashboard, approves proposals, team follow-ups, users and passwords, all reports.
SalesContacts, companies, deals, proposals, invoice/payment references, registrations, attendance, completion, feedback, tasks.
MarketingLists, segments, email and WhatsApp campaigns, automations, WhatsApp inbox, website forms.
RectorRead-only: executive dashboard, departments, reports.

New users get a short welcome tour on first login; the Guide page in the menu has the full step-by-step for every role and a glossary.
